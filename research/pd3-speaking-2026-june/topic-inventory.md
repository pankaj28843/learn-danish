## Officially Confirmed Topic Style

PD3 speaking topics are societal issues. Official Dansk og Prøver examples:

- Lifestyle diseases with focus on diabetes.
- The Danish school system with focus on bullying.
- Social media.
- Job search and unemployment.

Official written/reading topic examples are not speaking prompts, but they show the same PD3 topic style:

- Traffic safety.
- Workplace health offers.
- Entrepreneurship.
- Leadership.
- Disability.
