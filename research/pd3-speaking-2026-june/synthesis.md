## Exam Format

Official Dansk og Prøver describes the oral exam as two parts of about five minutes each:

- Part 1: prepared topic, given by the language center before the exam. The candidate gives a roughly two-minute presentation, then answers follow-up questions.
- Part 2: unseen topic drawn at the exam among three possible topics. The examiner asks concrete and broader follow-up questions.

Official examples for Part 1 include:

- Lifestyle diseases with a focus on diabetes.
- The Danish primary/lower-secondary school with a focus on bullying.

Official examples for Part 2 include:

- Social media.
- Job search and unemployment.

Clavis summarizes the operational format as about 15 minutes including grading, with Part 1 as 2 minutes presentation plus 3 minutes follow-up, and Part 2 as about 10 seconds to view two pictures plus around 4.5 minutes of questions.

## June 2026 Dates

Official Dansk og Prøver published that:

- Written PD3 is on 19 May 2026.
- Oral tests are from 10 June 2026 to 23 June 2026.

Studieskolen and UCplus independently confirm the oral period as 10-23 June 2026.

The official dates PDF says SIRI sends centrally prepared oral material to exam leaders on 1 June 2026, and that oral tests are held 10-23 June 2026. It also says evening tests can be held on 10, 16, 18, and 22 June 2026.

## Evidence Pattern

The same topic pattern appears across official pages, school pages, learner blogs, and prep-course marketing:

- The topic is rarely a narrow fact quiz.
- It asks for explanation, stance-taking, pros/cons, causes, consequences, and social perspective.
- Pictures in Part 2 are only the entry point; the heading/topic is the real task.
- A good answer moves from concrete observation to broader society.

## Preparation Implications

Do not try to memorize full speeches for 40 topics. Build reusable B2 answer frameworks:

- Cause/consequence: "En af årsagerne er...", "Det kan føre til..."
- Pros/cons: "På den ene side...", "På den anden side..."
- Society angle: "For samfundet betyder det..."
- Personal-to-general bridge: "Jeg har selv oplevet..., men hvis man ser bredere på det..."
- Clarifying question: "Mener du, om...?"

The Learn Danish PDF stresses structure, natural spoken language, short sentences, no full manuscript, timing practice, and strategic "planted" discussion hooks.
