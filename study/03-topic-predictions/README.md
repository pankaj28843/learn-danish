# Topic Predictions: June 2026

## TL;DR

- Most likely: work/stress, social media/digital life, health/welfare, school/youth, climate/transport.
- Medium likely: integration, equality/family, older people/pensions, housing/economy, crime/democracy.
- The prediction is a preparation map, not an official list.

## Prediction Ranking

### High Probability

1. Work, job search, unemployment, workplace stress.
2. Social media, children online, privacy, digital life.
3. Health, lifestyle disease, healthcare, welfare.
4. School, bullying, children, youth.
5. Climate, environment, transport, cycling.

### Medium Probability

6. Integration and immigration.
7. Equality, family, childcare, gender roles.
8. Older people, pensions, retirees.
9. Housing and personal economy.
10. Crime, punishment, democracy.

### Lower Wildcards

- Tourism.
- Entrepreneurship.
- Disability and inclusion.
- Public vs private sector.
- Volunteer work.
- Leadership.

## Why these predictions are reasonable

The high-probability groups appear in multiple evidence types:

- Official examples.
- Past learner reports.
- Course-prep pages.
- Exam-school marketing.
- Public practice-topic pages.

Work, digital life, health, school, and climate are especially useful to exam designers because they invite concrete picture description and abstract discussion.

## How to use this

Prepare five master answer frameworks:

1. Stress at work.
2. Social media and children.
3. Health prevention.
4. School and bullying.
5. Climate-friendly transport.

Then adapt them to neighboring topics.

## Hands-on exercises

1. Record a two-minute answer for each high-probability family.
2. After each recording, add one concrete example and one society-level point.
3. Practice two follow-up questions: "Hvorfor tror du...?" and "Hvilke fordele og ulemper...?"
