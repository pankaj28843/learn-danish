# Topic Inventory: The PD3 Speaking Universe

## TL;DR

- The official category is broad: ordinary and societal topics.
- Exact June 2026 topics are not public.
- Prepare topic families, not memorized speeches.
- Work, digital life, health, school/youth, climate, integration, equality, economy, and welfare are the core families.

## What is it?

This inventory is a map of the topics that can reasonably appear, based on public evidence. It combines official examples, past learner reports, teacher/course pages, and school marketing.

## High-yield topic families

### Work

- Jobsøgning og ledighed.
- Stress på arbejdspladsen.
- Fleksible arbejdstider.
- Arbejdsmiljø.
- At skifte job.
- Ligestilling på arbejdsmarkedet.

### Digital Life

- Sociale medier.
- Privatliv på nettet.
- Børn på nettet.
- Nyheder på sociale medier.
- Teknologi i hverdagen.
- Digital undervisning.

### Health and Welfare

- Livsstilssygdomme and diabetes.
- Sundhedstilbud på arbejdspladsen.
- Brugerbetaling.
- Ulighed i sundhed.
- Velfærdssamfundet.

### Children, School, and Youth

- Den danske folkeskole.
- Mobning.
- Børneinstitutioner.
- Unges økonomi.
- Unges alkoholvaner.

### Climate and Transport

- Klimabekymringer.
- Bæredygtige vaner.
- Cykelkultur.
- Trafiksikkerhed.

### Society and Integration

- Indvandring og integration.
- Statsborgerskab and participation.
- Demokrati.
- Frivilligt arbejde.
- Straf and alternative punishment.

## Why this matters

Most topics can be answered with the same B2 moves:

- Explain the problem.
- Give one reason.
- Give one consequence.
- Compare two sides.
- State your opinion.
- Lift to society.

## Hands-on exercises

1. Pick one topic from each family and write ten keywords.
2. For each topic, prepare one advantage and one disadvantage.
3. For each topic, prepare one "society sentence": "For samfundet betyder det..."
