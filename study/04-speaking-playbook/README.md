# Speaking Playbook: How to Sound B2

## TL;DR

- Use structure before complex vocabulary.
- Give reasons, contrasts, and consequences.
- Move from personal example to society.
- Ask for clarification instead of guessing wildly.
- Do not memorize a full manuscript.

## Part 1 Structure

Use this two-minute skeleton:

1. "Jeg vil tale om..."
2. "Først vil jeg..."
3. "Derefter vil jeg..."
4. "Til sidst vil jeg..."
5. Problem or conflict.
6. Two sides.
7. Your position.
8. Short conclusion.

Example angle for "social media":

- Problem: social media is useful but affects privacy and mental health.
- Side A: connection, news, community.
- Side B: pressure, misinformation, lost concentration.
- Opinion: children need guidance, not total bans.

## Part 2 Structure

When you see pictures:

1. Name the topic from the heading.
2. Describe the situation in one or two sentences.
3. Use topic vocabulary.
4. Answer the examiner's question directly.
5. Add cause, consequence, and society perspective.

## Useful Sentence Starters

- "En af årsagerne er..."
- "Det kan føre til..."
- "På den ene side..."
- "På den anden side..."
- "Efter min mening..."
- "Hvis man ser på samfundet som helhed..."
- "Det afhænger af..."
- "Jeg er ikke helt sikker, men jeg tror..."
- "Mener du, om...?"

## Pitfalls

- Too much memorized written Danish.
- Too many facts and not enough argument.
- Weak endings.
- Speaking too long in Part 1.
- Forgetting the examiner can ask about anything you introduce.

## Hands-on exercises

1. Pick a high-probability topic and write only keywords, not sentences.
2. Record yourself three times with different wording.
3. End your talk with a question hook: "Det rejser spørgsmålet om..."
4. Practice one clarification request in every mock exam.
